---- Turn order ----
1. Replenish: Draw to 3 cards
?10.Move Creatures which are on the board
?9. Move Enemy: Place/Move Heroes
2. Summon: Draw from your Domain until over
    1. Draw a Creature from your Domain
    2. Place the Creature at your Dungeon Hearth (or any other Room explicitly allowing it).
    3. Move the placed Creature.
        - [Creature] may move <= [Movement], as depicted on their Creature Type card.
        - [Creature] may only move along connected adjacent rooms(other cards may change this behaviour)
        - [Creature] must end their movement in a room with a free spot they can occupy. If there is no such movement possible it can't move and must occupy a spot in its current room. If that is also not possible the creature is returned to your Domain.
        - [Creature] only have to occupy a spot in the room where their movement ends. Not along the way
3. Activate: Activate any Number of Abilities on cards in your Dungeon
    1. Pay activation cost
        - See Rules->Costs
        - Cards that dont require any [Creature] as cost may be activated only during this phase and as often as one can pay the activation cost.
    2. Resolve Effect
4. Return: Return any number of [exhausted creature] on your Board to your Domain, pay [Food] for the rest
    1. Choose any number of [exhausted creature] in your dungeon and pay 1 [Food] for each of these creatures, flip these creatures [Creature]
    2. Return all other [exhausted creature] not choosen this way to your Domain
?5. Build: Place Rooms/Traps from Hand
    1. Pay Building Cost
        - See Rules->Costs
?6. Buy: Buy Cards and add them to Deck
?7. Recruit: Buy Creatures 
?8. Fight: Any number of [Creature] within a room with a hero fight that hero
    1. Fight
    2. The Hero plunders the room if no [Creature] are left in the room.


---- Rules ----
Costs: 
- Costs are defined in front of a ":" within the description of a card.
- Costs can be Resources like [Mana] or[Cards], they can be Creature types like [Demon][Undead][Wild][Magic] or [All].
    - If the Cost is a Resource it is paid by returning the corresponding amount of the resource, which is in your stash or stored in a room of your dungeon, to the Bank.
    - If the Cost is a Creature Type, it is paid by flipping a creature of the corresponding type in that room on the other side [exhausted creature].

Effect Resolving:
- Directly resolve any effect of paying cost, or triggered by effects before resolving other effects, 
eg creatures with abilities that trigger on exhaustion resolve before the ability for which the cost was paid resolves.
- If multiple effects you control would trigger at the same time you may choose their resolving order freely.

Basic Crature Type Boons:
- [Undead]: when this creature would die, return it to your domain instead.
- [Wild]: when adding a new [Wild] to your domain you may directly place that creature and move it.
- [Magic]: when a [Magic] is activated, add 1 [Mana]
- [Demon]: If you deal damage to a Hero and there is a [Demon] in the same room, deal 1 more damage instead.

Reserve:
- return any creatures in your reserve to your domain at the end of Activation.
