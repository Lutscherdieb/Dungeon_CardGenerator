- How does combat go?
    - (Combined defence of all your unexhausted creatures in the room) = X , (Combined defence of all enemies in the room) = Y 
    - X - Y = CombatValue (Can be negative)
    - 10 - CombatValue = Threshold for D20 roll
    - Roll D20 
    - if D20 value is lower than the Threshold your Creatures take damage equal to the difference distributed as you like not exceeding their individual [Health] valeus, if it is greater, your Enemies takes that much damage distributed as you like but nalso not exceeding their individual [Health] values.
    - Distribute the damage among your creatures not exceeding their individual [Health] Values, non-lethal damage to creatures does not need to be tracked. 
        - eg. 2 [Wild] unexhausted creatures with [Defence] 2 and [Health] 2 are in a Room with [Defence] + 1. One Hero with [Defence] 4 is also in the room.
        - 2+2+1 = 5 is your Defence. 4 = Enemy Defence.
        - 5-4 = 1
        - 10 - 1 = 9
        - D20 Roll = 7
        - 7 is lower than Thresold 9 so you take damage equal to the difference 9-7=2
        - Disribute the 2 damage among your creatures, one damage to each creature so both survive.
